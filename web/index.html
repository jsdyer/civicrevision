Coverage and collection limits

Checked every six hours. Each article request includes a bounded window of recent revisions; the oldest returned revision supplies a comparison baseline. Captures accumulate in monthly archives. An unusually busy article can outpace that window, and revision deletion or missed collection can leave gaps. This is not a complete Wikipedia archive.

    Article text is simplified from wikitext; long blocks may be shortened. Added/removed citation lists compare full ref bodies, so a changed citation may appear in both lists. Named citation reuses are not separate sources. Wikipedia reversion tags and content matches do not establish why a change happened.

    About the evidence

    Wikipedia is edited by volunteers and others. An edit about an institution is not evidence that the institution made it. This app does not identify editors or infer affiliations. Original revision links preserve the context and contributor attribution.

    Text and revision summaries: Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0. Wikimedia revision API. Data capture dates and revision dates are distinct.
